# Getting to Know My Tongue

## What do students do?

1. **Connect the parts of the tongue:** Students connect 6 cards to the right spots on the picture with ropes, then press “Check”. Correct cards get a tick and turn grey; wrong ones go back.
2. **The journey of taste:** Students connect 5 steps to the right numbers with ropes, then press “Check”.
3. **Quiz:** Students answer 5 questions from a pool of 18.
4. **Results:** Students see their result and can restart.

## Using it in an LMS

This package is **SCORM 2004**. Upload the ZIP file as it is, without unzipping it.

**Sent to the LMS:** the quiz score as a percentage, "passed" at 60% or more, and the time spent.
